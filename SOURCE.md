# Corresponding source

Catch Davel is distributed under the MIT License. The complete corresponding source for an official binary release is the exact Git tag and commit recorded in that release's `release-evidence.json` manifest.

The manifest also records the official HTTPS source URL and SHA-256 checksums for every released package. Obtain source from that URL or from the source archive published beside the packages. Modified or unofficial distributions must provide their own corresponding source and must not imply Quantum Billing endorsement.

If an official package is missing its evidence manifest or its recorded source cannot be obtained, contact the Quantum Billing project owner through the official release channel before redistributing it as an official build.
